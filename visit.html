Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I order?

Add wigs to your bag and tap “Send order on WhatsApp”. Your order arrives in our chat already written out. We confirm availability, colour and the total, then arrange payment and pickup or delivery with you.

Do you deliver outside Kasoa?

Yes, we deliver nationwide. The delivery fee depends on your location and is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay.

Is all your hair 100% human hair?

Yes. Each product lists its grade: double drawn (DD), super double drawn (SDD) or raw donor, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

What do SDD, 5×5 and glueless mean?

SDD (super double drawn) means most short strands are removed, so the hair is full from root to tip. 4×4 / 5×5 is the size of the lace closure in inches; bigger gives more parting space. Glueless units are secured with combs and an elastic band, so no adhesive is needed.

Can I see the hair before buying?

Absolutely. That is what the showroom is for. Visit us at Queens Plaza, Opeikuma Adom Junction, or ask for a video of the exact unit on WhatsApp.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. We supply salons, stylists and resellers. Message us on WhatsApp for the current wholesale price list and minimum quantities.

Why do some styles say “price on request”?

Some lengths and limited pieces change price with each shipment. Tap “Ask on WhatsApp” and we will reply with today’s price.
