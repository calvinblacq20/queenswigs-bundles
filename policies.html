Ordering

Our website is a catalogue and a quick way to send us your order. When you tap “Send order on WhatsApp”, your bag is written into a WhatsApp message to our official number, 024 164 8058. Nothing is charged on this website.

We reply to confirm stock, colour and the final total (including any delivery fee), then agree payment with you. Your order is only confirmed once we have both agreed in the chat.

  • Prices are in Ghana cedis (GH₵) and can change with each shipment.
  • Promotional prices apply only during the dates shown.
  • “Price on request” items are quoted in the chat.

Delivery & pickup

Pickup: collect from the showroom at Queens Plaza, Opeikuma Adom Junction, beside Radiance Petroleum, Kasoa, during opening hours (Mon–Sat 9am–8pm, Sun 10am–3pm).

Delivery: we deliver nationwide. The fee and expected timing depend on your location and are confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay.

Exchanges

Please check your hair carefully at pickup or on delivery. For hygiene reasons, wigs and bundles that have been worn, washed, cut, coloured or installed cannot be returned.

If something is wrong with your order, message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of receiving it with photos, and we will make it right with an exchange or repair where possible.

Privacy

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies and does not collect personal data on its servers.

  • Your bag is stored only in your own browser (local storage) so it is still there when you come back. Clear it any time by emptying your bag.
  • The name, area and note you type at checkout are placed into a WhatsApp message on your device. They reach us only if you choose to send that message.
  • We use your details only to process your order and contact you about it, in line with Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Ask us on WhatsApp or by email to see or delete any details we hold about you.
  • The map on our Visit page is provided by Google and follows Google’s privacy policy.
